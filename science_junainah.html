📚 Part 1: Interactive Core Notes

1. Sensory Receptors

Specialized neural structures detect energy changes in the environment:

  • Photoreceptors: Light detection (Eyes)
  • Mechanoreceptors: Touch, pressure, sound waves (Skin/Ears)
  • Chemoreceptors: Chemical signals (Tongue/Nose)

2. Life Processes (MRS GREN)

All 7 characteristics must be present for an organism to be classified as living:

M - Movement | R - Respiration | S - Sensitivity
G - Growth | R - Reproduction | E - Excretion | N - Nutrition
