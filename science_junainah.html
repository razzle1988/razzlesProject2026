🧠 Interactive Notes & Concepts

1. Sensory Receptors & Nervous System

Receptors detect environmental stimuli (photoreceptors for light, mechanoreceptors for touch/sound, chemoreceptors for taste/smell). Signal pulses travel through sensory neurons to the brain for decoding.

2. Living vs. Non-Living (MRS GREN)

To classify an organism as living, it must satisfy all 7 characteristics: Movement, Respiration, Sensitivity, Growth, Reproduction, Excretion, and Nutrition.
