What Hessa Will Learn

  • Name the five main senses.
  • Identify the body part (sense organ) for each sense.
  • Describe what each sense tells us.
  • Explain that the brain is the boss that reads all messages.
  • Understand how our senses keep us safe.

Topic 1: Big Idea + Sight (Eyes) & Pupil Experiment

Day 1

Lesson Notes:

Your body is finding out about the world right now! You see with your eyes, hear with your ears, smell with your nose, taste with your tongue, and feel with your skin.

These five special body parts are called sense organs. They collect clues about everything around you and send tiny electrical messages along pathways called nerves straight to your brain. Your brain is the boss that reads all these messages and tells you what you are experiencing!

Focus on Sight: Your eyes take in light. The black dot in the center of your eye is called the pupil. It lets light enter your eye so you can see pictures of the world!

🧪 Experiment: The Expanding Pupil

1. Look at your eyes in a mirror in a brightly lit room. Notice how big or small your pupil is.
2. Dim the lights or cover your eyes gently with your hands for 10 seconds.
3. Look in the mirror as you turn the light back on!

Topic 2: Hearing (Ears), Sound Walk & Vibration Experiments

Day 2

Lesson Notes:

Your ears catch sounds! Sounds are actually created by tiny wiggles in the air called vibrations. When an object vibrates, it pushes air in invisible waves, which travel inside your ear canal and hit your eardrum!

🧪 Experiment: Seeing Vibrations

1. Stretch plastic wrap tightly over a plastic tub or bowl.
2. Sprinkle a few grains of uncooked rice on top.
3. Snap a thick rubber band or hum loudly right next to the bowl!

🎧 Activity: 5-Minute Sound Walk

Sit quietly for 5 minutes inside or outdoors. Close your eyes and listen carefully!

Topic 3: Smell (Nose) & Taste (Tongue) Investigations

Day 3

Lesson Notes:

Your nose and tongue work together as a team! Inside your nose are tiny smell receptors that pick up scent particles floating in the air. On your tongue are tiny bumps called taste buds.

Your taste buds can recognize four main primary tastes: Sweet, Sour, Salty, and Bitter. When you eat, your nose and taste buds send messages to your brain at the same time to create the full flavor!

👃 Investigation 1: Smell Jars

Dip cotton balls into different items (vanilla extract, vinegar, lemon juice, cinnamon) and place them in numbered cups. Hold them under your nose with your eyes closed!

Topic 4: Touch (Skin), Mystery Bag & Safety

Day 4

Lesson Notes:

Your skin is the largest sense organ on your body! All over your skin are tiny feelers called nerve endings. They tell your brain if something is hot, cold, soft, rough, smooth, or sharp.

Senses Keep Us Safe: Our senses warn us of danger! Your eyes see red stop lights, your ears hear car horns or fire alarms, your skin pulls back from a hot stove, and your nose smells smoke or spoiled food before you eat it.

✋ Investigation: Mystery Bag Challenge

Reach into a bag containing hidden items without looking!
